About Catscore

Catscore is a small community site for people who believe that life is better with a cat nearby.

We collect little stories from everyday life: the first warm day by an open window, a new cardboard-box discovery, or the familiar footsteps that mean it is nearly dinner time.

This site is a work in progress. For now, take a look around, read a few comments, and enjoy the quiet company.

Our unofficial principles

  • Curiosity is always welcome.
  • Every sunbeam deserves investigation.
  • There is no such thing as too many photos.
